1. **Thermodynamic Extreme Discrepancy**: Chandipur median CAPE ($763.4\text{ J/kg}$) is **21.7 times higher** than Florida median CAPE ($35.2\text{ J/kg}$), and its maximum reaches $9,906\text{ J/kg}$, extending $2,666\text{ J/kg}$ beyond the Florida training boundary.
2. **Deep Convective Inversion**: Chandipur exhibits substantial capping inversion (target median $\text{CIN} = 56.3\text{ J/kg}$ vs Florida median $0.0\text{ J/kg}$ with $59.7\%$ zero values in Florida).
3. **Kinematic Enclosure**: Target bulk shear values ($[0, 56.45]\text{ m/s}$) are completely contained within the Florida support ($[0, 77.78]\text{ m/s}$), demonstrating that kinematics do not suffer from out-of-domain extrapolation issues.
